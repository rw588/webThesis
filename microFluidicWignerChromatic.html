Gaussian-Schell Model — Polychromatic microfluidic Talbot Carpet

\[ \langle I_{\text{microfluidic}}\rangle(x,L+z) = \int\!\tilde{A}(v)\,I_{\text{microfluidic}}\big(x,L+z;\,\lambda_{\rm dB}(v)\big)\,dv, \qquad \tilde{A}(v)=\mathcal{N}(v_0,\sigma_v^2), \qquad \lambda_{\rm dB}(v)=\frac{h}{m_{\rm Mu}v}, \qquad z_T(v)=\frac{2d^2}{\lambda_{\rm dB}(v)}\propto v \] \[ \begin{aligned} I_{\text{microfluidic}}(x,L+z) &= I_0\frac{\sigma_I(0)}{\sigma_I(L+z)}\sum_{m,n} a_m a_n^* \underbrace{e^{i\frac{2\pi}{d}(m-n)x}}_{\text{spatial fringes}} \underbrace{e^{-i\pi\frac{\lambda z}{d^2}(m^2-n^2)}}_{\text{Talbot phase}} \\[8pt] &\quad\times \underbrace{\exp\!\left[-\frac{\left(x-\frac{m\lambda z}{d}\right)^2+\left(x-\frac{n\lambda z}{d}\right)^2}{4\sigma_I^2(L+z)}\right]}_{\text{displaced intensity envelopes}} \\[8pt] &\quad\times \underbrace{\exp\!\left[-\frac{(m-n)^2\lambda^2z^2}{2\sigma_c^2(L+z)d^2}\right]}_{\text{transverse coherence}} \\[8pt] &\quad\times \underbrace{\exp\!\left[\frac{ik}{2R(L+z)}\left(\left(x-\frac{m\lambda z}{d}\right)^2-\left(x-\frac{n\lambda z}{d}\right)^2\right)\right]}_{\text{wavefront-curvature phase}} \\&\quad\times \sum_{s=-\infty}^{\infty} \underbrace{\exp\left[-\frac{s^2d^2}{2\sigma_I^2(L+z)}+\frac{sdx}{\sigma_I^2(L+z)}-\frac{s(m+n)\lambda z}{2\sigma_I^2(L+z)}+i\frac{2\pi s (m-n)z}{R(L+z)}\right]}_{\text{all source-position dependence}} \end{aligned} \]

Velocity average: with \(v=v_0(1+\delta)\), each term's phase \(\Psi\) and envelope centre are expanded to first order in \(\delta\) and averaged over the Gaussian in closed form, e.g. \(\langle e^{i\Psi}\rangle \approx e^{i\Psi(v_0)}\,e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left(\partial_\delta\Psi\right)^2\sigma_v^2/v_0^2}\).
